The establishment of this company came from the experience one had many years ago. We are passionate when it comes to water adventures. Our motto has always been "Your satisfaction, our priority" and our customers know that. Our mission is to make sure that people are accustomed with water adventures

History

Mountain View Rafting Company was established in 1992 by two partners. The company had one office by then, but grew to be a multinational company with over two hundred branches all over the world.

The company started by offering only one service ot touring water bodies. As of 2020, more services were added including lodging and restaurants for those who wish to spend more days.
